# 🚨 Key Risk Observations

## 1️⃣ Patch Management Delays
Critical vulnerabilities were not consistently remediated within defined timelines.

### Governance Concern
Delayed remediation increases exposure to exploitation and operational disruption.

### Recommendation
Implement formal patch governance tracking and remediation SLAs.

---

## 2️⃣ Incomplete Vendor Governance Documentation
Evidence of periodic governance reviews was limited.

### Governance Concern
Reduced audit defensibility and oversight visibility.

### Recommendation
Establish documented governance review procedures.
