# 🏛️ Vendor Risk Assessment Methodology

## 📌 Purpose

This document defines a governance-oriented methodology for assessing third-party/vendor cybersecurity and technology risks.

The objective is to ensure vendors are evaluated using consistent, risk-based criteria aligned with enterprise governance, operational resilience, compliance, and information security expectations.

---

# 🎯 Governance Objectives

- Identify third-party security risks
- Evaluate inherent and residual vendor risk
- Improve vendor governance oversight
- Support audit and compliance readiness
- Enable risk-informed vendor onboarding decisions

---

# 🔍 Vendor Risk Assessment Lifecycle

## 1️⃣ Vendor Identification
Third parties are identified based on:
- Business dependency
- Data access level
- System integration
- Regulatory exposure
- Operational criticality

---

## 2️⃣ Inherent Risk Evaluation
Initial vendor risk is assessed before controls are considered.

Risk factors include:
- Sensitive data handling
- Cloud service usage
- External connectivity
- Access privileges
- Geographic/legal exposure
- Regulatory obligations

---

## 3️⃣ Security Control Assessment
The following controls may be evaluated:

| Control Area | Assessment Focus |
|---|---|
| Access Management | MFA, privileged access, IAM |
| Logging & Monitoring | SIEM visibility, audit logging |
| Vulnerability Management | Patch governance |
| Incident Response | Vendor IR maturity |
| Data Protection | Encryption & DLP |
| Compliance | ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA |

---

## 4️⃣ Residual Risk Evaluation
Residual risk is evaluated after considering:
- Existing vendor controls
- Security certifications
- Governance maturity
- Compensating controls

---

## 5️⃣ Risk Treatment Decision
Possible outcomes include:
- Vendor approval
- Conditional approval
- Risk acceptance
- Additional remediation requirements
- Vendor rejection

---

# 📊 Sample Vendor Risk Ratings

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| Critical | Severe operational/regulatory exposure |
| High | Significant business impact |
| Medium | Moderate operational risk |
| Low | Limited business exposure |

# 👥 Governance Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Vendor Risk Team | Assessment coordination |
| Security Team | Security control validation |
| Compliance Team | Regulatory review |
| Business Owners | Vendor sponsorship |
| Leadership | Risk acceptance decisions |

# 📈 Governance Benefits

This methodology helps organizations:
- Improve vendor oversight
- Reduce third-party exposure
- Strengthen audit defensibility
- Support regulatory expectations
- Improve operational resilience

Aligned with:
